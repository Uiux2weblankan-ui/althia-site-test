Nursing

Nursing that starts at the bedside.

Our nursing courses pair theory with hands-on practice from the first semester. You train in simulation suites on campus, then build confidence on real wards through supervised rotations at Hemas Hospitals.

Choose from certificate, diploma and higher diploma routes, including a Higher Diploma delivered with Deakin University that opens an international pathway.

FAQ

Nursing questions, answered.

Can't find your answer? Our admissions team is happy to help.

Ask admissions →
What's the difference between the HND and the Higher Diploma (Deakin)?
The HND in Nursing is awarded by ALTHIA / SLIIT. The Higher Diploma in Nursing is delivered with Deakin University and opens a pathway to continue your studies in Australia.
Where do clinical rotations take place?
At Hemas Hospitals, under the supervision of clinical instructors and ward staff.
Can I start without A/Ls?
The Diploma in Nursing Assistance and the Certificate in Patient Care are entry routes into healthcare. Ask admissions about progression.
Are scholarships available?
Scholarships are offered for the HND in Nursing (NVQ L6). Contact admissions for the current scheme.
What are the entry requirements?
Requirements differ by course. See the admissions page or ask our team.
