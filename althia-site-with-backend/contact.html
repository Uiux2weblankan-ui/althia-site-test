FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Quick answers before you get in touch. Still unsure? Call, WhatsApp or send the form above.

How do I apply?
Applications are made online through SLIIT's application portal. Start your application, or read our admissions guide.
Can I visit the campus before applying?
Yes. Book a campus visit with the enquiry form above, or join our next Open Day.
What are your office hours?
Monday to Friday, 8:30 am – 5:00 pm, and Saturday, 9:00 am – 1:00 pm. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
How quickly will you reply?
We reply within one working day. For a quicker answer, message us on WhatsApp.
Do you offer scholarships or instalment plans?
Yes, for selected courses, including scholarships for the HND in Nursing. Ask our admissions team for the current options.
Can international or overseas-qualified students apply?
Yes. Send us your qualifications and we'll check them for you before you apply.
