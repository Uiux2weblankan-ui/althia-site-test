AboutALTHIA.

A SLIIT and Hemas collaboration for the next generation of healthcare professionals.

Our story

Two trusted names, one purpose.

ALTHIA Health Uni (Private) Limited is a collaboration between SLIIT and Hemas Holdings, developing the next generation of healthcare professionals through innovation, excellence and global standards.

SLIIT brings decades of academic experience. Hemas brings real hospitals, real patients and an understanding of what the healthcare sector needs. Students learn at the SLIIT Malabe Campus, practise in simulation labs and train on the ward at Hemas Hospitals.
