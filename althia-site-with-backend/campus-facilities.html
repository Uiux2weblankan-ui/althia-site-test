Campus overview

A 25-acre smart campus.

ALTHIA sits within the SLIIT Malabe campus, a 25-acre green setting just outside Colombo. Every building is connected, every lab is current, and quiet spaces are never far away.

You train in the same kind of rooms you will work in, from simulated hospital wards to research-grade labs, with Hemas Hospitals clinicians close at hand.
