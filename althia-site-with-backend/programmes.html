Overview

Degrees built around real practice.

Every ALTHIA programme combines classroom learning, simulation labs and supervised placements, so you graduate ready for the ward, the clinic or the lab from day one.

Choose a programme type below to see all the courses it offers. Some programmes are awarded with partner universities and open international pathways.

  • Academic rigour

    Curricula and standards shaped by SLIIT, one of Sri Lanka's leading higher-education institutes.

  • Clinical practice

    Simulation suites on campus and clinical rotations at Hemas Hospitals.

  • Global pathways

    Programmes with international partners, including a Higher Diploma in Nursing with Deakin University.
